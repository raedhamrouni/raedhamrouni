## 🧬 Who Am I?

> *"I don't just use AI — I engineer it into production."*

I'm **Raed Mohamed Amine Hamrouni**, a **Fullstack AI Engineer at Medinfotec, Portugal** 🇵🇹, and a June 2026 graduate with an Engineering degree in **Data Science & AI** from the Polytechnic School of Sousse 🇹🇳 and a Master's in Web Services & Multimedia.

I build and ship **end-to-end AI systems** — from agentic LLM pipelines and MLOps infrastructure to full-stack products with **Next.js**, React, Node.js, FastAPI, and Docker. I've deployed AI to **live production platforms**, integrated **Gemini** and **LangGraph** for multi-tenant AI agent platforms with real-time **voice/telephony** (LiveKit, ElevenLabs), built **RAG systems** with LangChain and FAISS, and fine-tuned **Llama 3.2 with LoRA**.
